# The Best Advice I Ever Got

A single-sentence entry for the make.fun bounty: one line about the best
advice the writer ever received, told through a real person in a real
moment — a grandmother at the stove, a wooden spoon worn smooth by forty
years of use, and a piece of advice about choosing a partner that holds
up far beyond the kitchen.

`main.py` writes that one sentence (under 200 characters) to
`/output/entry.txt`.
